Annotation Scheme and How to Use

Annotation types

SPK - Speech turn. Text = transcript of what was said.

SIL - Silence/pause. Text = pause notation: (.) <200ms, (0.3) 200-500ms, (0.7) 500ms-1s, (1.2) 1-2s, (2.5) >2s. Auto-inserted by Auto-fill SIL button.

BRE - Audible breath.

FP - Filler / hesitation: uh, um, er, hm.

LAU - Laughter.

BCK - Backchannel: mmh, yeah, right, okay, mm-hmm...

OVL - Overlap. Subtype auto-set by Classify OVL or set manually.

NOI - Noise / artefact.

MOD - Moderator-only event: [INSTRUCTION], [WARNING].

OVL subtypes (timing + lexical cues)

simultaneous both starts <200ms apart | smooth short overlap (<300ms) | backchannel_ovl short supportive response | collaborative supportive completion/continuation | competitive interruption with conflict cue | floor_fight sustained overlap with conflict cues | needs_review ambiguous case

Setting precise boundaries - the key workflow

1. Click a row to select it. Boundary bar shows onset / end / duration.

2. Play audio. Press S the moment you hear the utterance START. Onset is updated.

3. Continue playing. Press E the moment you hear the utterance END. Duration updated.

4. Move to next row, repeat.

Adding backchannels mid-sentence

Press B when you hear the backchannel - playhead is captured, type set to BCK. Pick speaker, click a preset or type text, press Enter.

Auto-fill SIL

Click Auto-fill SIL in the header. Finds every gap between consecutive SPK entries and inserts SIL entries with correct pause notation.

Auto-classify OVL

Click Classify OVL. Detects all overlapping SPK pairs and creates OVL entries with auto-classified subtypes.

Pause notation

Parentheses with value: (.) under 200ms, then actual seconds to 1 decimal place: (0.3), (1.5), etc.
